# Interface references

Flex uses an independent visual identity with a Paytm-inspired navy (#002970), cyan (#00BAF2), white and cool-blue surface palette. These are implementation choices inspired by the brand appearance, not a claim to certified brand specifications or Paytm affiliation. No Paytm logo, bank connection or integration is included.

Reference applications and the patterns used:

- [Paytm Spend Summary](https://paytm.com/blog/payment-gateway/paytm-spend-summary-a-simple-way-to-understand-your-spending/): monthly overview and category-level spending clarity. Flex keeps monthly financial totals and categories easy to scan.
- [Jupiter Pots](https://jupiter.money/pots/): goals give saving a concrete purpose. Flex emphasises the user's selected savings goal; it does not create a deposit account or move funds into a pot.
- [Fi connected-account overview](https://fi.money/features/connect-all-your-bank-accounts): consolidated context supports understanding money. Flex brings its seeded monthly state and scenario comparison into one workspace; it does not imply accounts are connected.

Design changes: navy primary actions and savings figures, cyan comparison bars and section accents, cool-blue information panels, pill-style scenario tabs and softly elevated cards. All scenario path cards have equal visual treatment so a decorative accent does not imply a recommended choice.

The audience remains people with predictable salary income exploring discretionary purchases against a savings goal. CIRCLES prioritisation still favours understandable consequences and user protections; Double Diamond research remains pending. These references inform interface patterns, not evidence of user demand or a complete competitive assessment.
