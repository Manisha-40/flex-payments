01 · THE PROBLEM

The decision happens before the transaction.

Most financial interfaces become useful after money moves. The unresolved moment is earlier: “If I choose this, what changes?”

01

History is retrospective

Statements and spend summaries categorize what already happened. They help reflection, but the next choice still requires mental arithmetic.

02

Budgets flatten priorities

A generic “cut discretionary spend” response can ignore what matters: dinners with friends, family support, commute or health.

03

Advice can overreach

Prescriptive AI can sound certain despite incomplete data. Users need consequences and assumptions, then retain the decision.

08 · RISK & FUTURE

A partnership comes after proof.

Paytm is a plausible future environment because it combines transaction context, consumer reach and an AI-led product direction. That is strategic fit—not evidence of product-market fit.

DATA COMPLETENESS

Partial history can mislead.

Show assumptions, allow edits, display confidence, and avoid affordability claims until coverage is sufficient.

Mitigation · editable state + uncertainty
AI RELIABILITY

Language models can invent.

Restrict Gemini to structured intent. Validate its schema and let the deterministic engine calculate.

Mitigation · separation of duties
REGULATORY PERIMETER

Decision support can sound like advice.

Block investment, lending and insurance recommendations. Present unranked paths and factual consequences.

Mitigation · scope boundary + review
BEHAVIOURAL HARM

Rewards can encourage spending.

Reward learning and consistency only. Let users control reminders and never create artificial urgency.

Mitigation · ethical reward rules
